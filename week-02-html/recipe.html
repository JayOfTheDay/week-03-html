Chocolate Chip Cookies

Step

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a bowl, cream together 1 cup of softened butter, 1 cup of white sugar, and 1 cup of brown sugar until smooth.
  3. Beat in 2 eggs one at a time, then stir in 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract.
  4. In a separate bowl, combine 3 cups of all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon of baking soda, and 1/2 teaspoon of salt. Gradually blend this into the creamed mixture.
  5. Stir in 2 cups of chocolate chips.
  6. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets.
  7. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, or until edges are nicely browned. Cool on wire racks.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips
